Design

A distinctive form in northern Bucharest.

Concept

Proportions. Light. Material.

The asymmetric mansard roofline creates a residential character rarely seen in Bucharest's new construction. Inspired by contemporary European residential typologies — Belgian, Nordic, Northern French — the buildings are calm, breathable, and unmistakably designed. Two volumes, one identity.

Location

Northern Bucharest's most liveable quarter.

Herăstrău – Aviației. Minutes from the park, connected to the business district, close to international schools and the airport. A calm residential address in the best-served part of the city.

  • Herăstrău Park
    5 min walk — largest urban park in Bucharest
  • Pipera business district
    8 min drive — Oracle, Vodafone, SAP campuses
  • International schools
    5–12 min — Noblesse, Cambridge School, Mark Twain
  • Henri Coandă International Airport
    20 min drive via DN1 / A3
  • Promenada Mall / Băneasa Shopping City
    10 min — dining, retail, cinema
  • A1 / A3 motorway access
    12 min — direct connection north and west

Neighborhood
Aviației is one of Bucharest's most sought-after residential addresses — low-traffic streets, mature trees, embassies, and immediate access to Herăstrău Park. Established, discreet, and consistently in demand.
