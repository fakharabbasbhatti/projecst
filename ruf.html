About Titima SA

The Largest Private Tanker Owner in Cameroon

Titima SA is a Cameroon-incorporated shipowning company headquartered in Douala, Bonanjo — the commercial and maritime heart of Central Africa's largest port. The company owns and operates three medium-range product tankers, making it the dominant private tanker owner in the country.

Our vessels are maintained to the highest international standards under the technical management of Unibros Shipping Corp, a Piraeus-based ship management company with deep expertise in product tanker operations. All vessels are classed with Bureau Veritas and carry full IMO Type 2 certification for the carriage of a wide range of liquid bulk products.

Regional Presence

Rooted in Cameroon,
Operating Internationally

Titima SA was founded with the ambition of building a world-class tanker fleet under Cameroonian ownership. Incorporated and headquartered in Douala — the gateway to Central Africa — the company represents a flagship investment in maritime infrastructure for the country and region.

The Port of Douala handles the majority of Cameroon's trade flows and serves as a key hub for landlocked neighbours Chad and the Central African Republic. Titima's vessels are positioned to serve the full spectrum of regional energy and petrochemical logistics, while operating freely in international markets.

All three vessels fly the Cameroonian flag, reinforcing the company's commitment to the country's maritime registry and contributing to the development of a national tanker fleet.

Operations & Safety

Committed to the Highest
Standards at Sea

Technical Excellence

Managed by Unibros Shipping Corp (Piraeus), with decades of product tanker experience and a rigorous preventive maintenance programme aligned with ISM Code requirements.

Bureau Veritas Class

All vessels maintain continuous Bureau Veritas classification with up-to-date statutory surveys, ISM, ISPS, and MLC certification held in full compliance.

Environmental Compliance

Two vessels are fitted with EGCS (exhaust gas cleaning scrubbers) for sulphur oxide reduction. All vessels comply with MARPOL Annexes I, II, and VI and operate under strict ballast water management plans.

SIRE & Port State

Vessels undergo regular SIRE inspections (OCIMF) and maintain strong Port State Control records. Our commitment to inspection readiness ensures minimal delay and high vetting acceptance rates.

Double Hull Construction

All three vessels are fully double-hulled in line with MARPOL 73/78 requirements, providing enhanced protection against accidental pollution and structural integrity throughout their operational life.

Professional Crews

Experienced, STCW-certified multinational crews managed through Unibros Shipping. Crew welfare and competency are central to our operational philosophy, with continuous training and certification management.
